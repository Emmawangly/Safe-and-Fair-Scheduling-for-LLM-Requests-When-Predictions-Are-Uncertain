This paper propose a scheduler that incorporate prediction uncertainty and aging mechanism to improve the scheduling decisions for LLM serving. Unlike approach that optimized for single operating point, the proposed scheduler was tuned and evaluate across every tested prediction-error level, to achieve robust performance under different prediction condition. In a normal load scenario, this approach reduce job completion time by 42.7\% relative to FCFS, while keeping the starvation below 1\%, and it stay close to this level of stability even under out-of-distribution prediction failure, where baseline predictor-based scheduler degrade way more. These result suggest that accounting for prediction uncertainty during the scheduling is a practical way to improve the robustness without giving up the fairness in real LLM serving systems.
